Callee buffers the offer + any ICE candidates until the user taps Accept, then sets remote SDP, answers, emits `call:accept` with its answer, flushes buffered candidates. Caller applies the answer on `call:accept`. 30s no-answer → caller emits `call:cancel` + "No answer". Busy → auto `call:reject`. `call:unavailable` → "Not available right now". Notices/toasts 3.5s.
